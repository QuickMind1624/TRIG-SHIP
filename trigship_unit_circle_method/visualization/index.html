Unit Circle — Interactive Visualization

Move A and B. Watch the point at A+B and its coordinates change.

The circle has radius 1. The blue radius points to A; the gold radius points to A+B; the dashed red chord joins the two points. Coordinates are shown exactly as trigonometric expressions and approximately as decimals for visual checking.

Identities connected to the picture

cos(A+B) = cos A cos B − sin A sin B
sin(A+B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin B
cos(A−B) = cos A cos B + sin A sin B
sin(A−B) = sin A cos B − cos A sin B
